  During the pandemic I worked on epidemiological modelling of COVID-19 as part of the Royal Society RAMP (Rapid Assistance in Modelling the Pandemic) initiative, for which I received the RAMP Early Career Investigator Award.
---

With the Cambridge Soft Matter group, I helped develop [PyRoss](https://github.com/rajeshrinet/pyross), an open-source Python library for inference, forecasts and optimal control of epidemiological models. On the methods side, we developed efficient Bayesian inference for fully stochastic epidemiological models, making it possible to fit compartmental models to noisy case data while properly accounting for demographic stochasticity — and applied this to COVID-19 (Li, Y. I. et al., *Royal Society Open Science*, 2021). This work connected naturally to my interests in stochastic dynamics and non-equilibrium statistical physics.
