We are a **theoretical biophysics** group interested in signalling and morphogenesis.  
{:.lede}

Biological systems operate far from equilibrium, continuously consuming energy to generate the shape changes and the wildy fluctuating signals that drive development. Using the tools of non-equilibrium statistical physics, and working hand in hand with experimental collaborators, we build minimal, physically grounded theories that connect microscopic interactions to the emergent behaviours of cells and tissues.
